How It Works

From leaf to valve, in three steps

No cloud dependency in the field. The spectroscope talks to your phone over Bluetooth Low Energy; your phone syncs to the cloud whenever it has a connection.

01

Calibrate & Scan

Run a dark-current and white-reference calibration, then clip the sensor onto a leaf. The AS7265x captures 18 channels across visible and near-infrared light.

02

On-Device Chemometrics

The app computes NDVI, NDRE, water band index, chlorophyll index and a vitality score directly from the raw spectrum — no server round-trip required to see a result.

03

Sync & Irrigate

Scans queue locally if you're offline and sync automatically once connected. Vitality and water-stress signals feed the 6-zone irrigation controller's recommendations.

Irrigation

Closed-loop watering across 6 zones

Soil moisture, temperature and salinity telemetry from each zone, with bi-stable solenoid valves you can trigger manually, on a schedule, or from spectroscopy-driven recommendations.

6 Independent Zones

Each zone has its own soil sensor cluster and solenoid gate, managed from one dashboard.

Bi-Stable Solenoid Valves

Low-power latching valves hold their open/closed state without continuous current draw.

Emergency Killswitch

One tap closes every valve across all 6 zones immediately — and locally, even without a network connection.

Offline Failsafe

If the network drops mid-irrigation, zones fail safe to closed locally rather than waiting on the cloud.

Technology

Built offline-first, synced securely

Every scan is stored locally the moment it's captured, then synced to Cloud Firestore automatically once your phone has a connection — with no scan ever lost to a dropped signal.
